# Scenarios

A scenario is the *setting* only: where the two people are and how they came to
be talking. It never says what either of them wants — that's an objective, in
`objectives.md`. Any scenario can be run with any pair of objectives.

Each scenario is a `##` heading (the name you pass to `--scenario`) with these
`###` sections:

| Section | Required | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| `Tagline` | no | nobody — it's for the menu and `--list` |
| `Setting` | **yes** | both people |
| `First` | no | only the first speaker (the one who opens) |
| `Second` | no | only the second speaker |
| `Knowledge` | no | both — what they may assume they already know |
| `Opener` | **yes** | only the first speaker, only on turn 1 |

`{partner}` is the other person's name and `{speaker}` is their own. Use `First`
and `Second` when the two sides aren't symmetric (host and guest). Use
`Knowledge` to say whether they're strangers: without it, two personas will
happily invent a shared history.

Keep it short. A long setting crowds out the persona and the objective.

## online

### Tagline
Chatting online, never met, nothing but text

### Setting
You and {partner} are chatting online. You've never met or seen each other, so all you have to go on is what you type to each other.

### Knowledge
You know nothing about {partner}'s life except what {partner} has told you in this chat.

### Opener
(You've just started chatting with {partner}. Open with a casual discussion about where they are and what's around them right now.)

## hosting

### Tagline
One of them is hosting the other at home for the evening

### Setting
It's a weekday evening. There's food, something to drink, and no particular reason to hurry.

### First
You're at home, and {partner} has come over for the evening. It's your kitchen, your food, your music.

### Second
You're at {partner}'s place for the evening. You brought something with you. You're comfortable here but it isn't your house.

### Opener
(You've just let {partner} in and poured them something. Open with whatever you say to someone who has just arrived.)

## first-meeting

### Tagline
Just introduced at a mutual friend's party

### Setting
You and {partner} have just been introduced at a mutual friend's party and left to talk. You're standing near the kitchen with a drink each. The room is loud enough to be annoying.

### Knowledge
You have never met {partner} before and know nothing about them beyond their name. Whoever introduced you didn't explain either of you.

### Opener
(You've just been introduced to {partner} and the person who introduced you has wandered off. Open the way someone does when they've been left with a stranger.)

## delayed-flight

### Tagline
Stuck next to each other, four hours and counting

### Setting
You and {partner} are in adjacent seats at an airport gate. Your flight has been delayed four hours with no explanation. Everyone around you has given up and gone quiet. Neither of you can leave.

### Knowledge
You'd never seen {partner} before today and you only know what you've overheard at this gate.

### Opener
(The board has just pushed the delay back another hour. Open with whatever you say to the stranger next to you when that happens.)

## old-friends

### Tagline
Catching up properly after years apart

### Setting
You and {partner} have known each other for years but haven't talked properly in a long time. You're catching up with no agenda and plenty of time.

### Knowledge
You have a long shared history with {partner}. You can refer to things you've both done before, as long as you stay consistent about them once you've said them.

### Opener
(It's been years. Open by picking up wherever you think you left off.)

## late-shift

### Tagline
The only two awake in the building

### Setting
You and {partner} are the only two people still in the building, hours after everyone else left. You're each waiting on something that isn't finished. It's the kind of tired where people say more than they mean to.

### Opener
(You've both just admitted you're not going home any time soon. Open with something about the hour or the waiting.)
